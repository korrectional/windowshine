# WindowShine — Modernist Glasshouse Design System

## 1. Aesthetic Thesis & Visual Language
The Modernist Glasshouse aesthetic rejects clutter, blue gradient clichés, and commodity handyman templates. It treats exterior residential glass cleaning through the lens of modern architectural clarity:
- **Structural Grid & Rules:** Disciplined hairlines, razor-sharp alignment, and generous breathing room mirroring high-end modern glass pavilions.
- **High-Contrast Palette:** Deep obsidian navy (`#050814`, `#0b1226`), pure glasshouse white (`#ffffff`, `#f8fafc`), clean sky reflection cerulean (`#0284c7`), and selective Carolina sunlight amber (`#f59e0b`).
- **Architectural Typography:** Bold structural display lettering (`Plus Jakarta Sans` -0.03em) anchored by clear geometric reading measure (`Figtree`).

## 2. Layout & Composition
- **Asymmetric Architectural Framing:** Balancing editorial typography against authentic residential photography.
- **Hairline Panes & Grids:** Cards and structural blocks act like architectural window panes separated by disciplined hairline borders.
- **High Visual Contrast:** Sharp transitions between luminous white glass sections and deep dusk obsidian structural blocks.

## 3. Strict Project Constraints
- **100% Exterior Residential:** Zero indoor intrusion. Homeowners do not need to be home.
- **Zero Water Science Claims:** No deionized water, pure water tech, or TDS claims.
- **Zero Legalistic Guarantees:** No rain warranties or insurance marketing claims. Work ethics expressed through human standards (On-Time, Complete Scope, Clean Grounds).
- **Simplified Form:** 5 inputs only (Date, Full Name, Phone, Address, Zip) connected to Google Sheets with anti-spam honeypot.
- **Bot-Screened Phone Access:** Non-breaking phone numbers with human event verification.
