## Users

Homeowners and residential property managers in Charlotte, NC and surrounding areas (Myers Park, Ballantyne, Dilworth, SouthPark, Plaza Midwood, Lake Norman, Matthews, Huntersville, Waxhaw) who value pristine, streak-free natural light in their homes and want a reliable, professional, insured service without hassle or aggressive upselling.

## Product Purpose

WindowShine delivers residential exterior and interior window cleaning, screen washing, and track detailing in Charlotte, NC. The website exists to convert local homeowners into scheduled appointments through transparent information, proof of craftsmanship (pure-water pole wash, streak-free guarantee), and a frictionless lead contact and estimate booking form.

## Positioning

Unlike generic multi-service handymen, WindowShine specializes exclusively in residential glass perfection. We use purified deionized water that dries 100% spotless without mineral deposits, pair it with drop-cloth protected interior hand-squeegee technique, and back every job with a 48-Hour Rain Guarantee and $2,000,000 liability insurance.

## Operating Context

Homeowners browsing on mobile phones or desktops after noticing dirty seasonal pollen, construction dust, or hard water spots; requesting a quote or appointment during work or in the evening; expecting prompt callback, transparent estimates, and punctual technicians wearing clean uniforms and booties indoors.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Scope: Residential only (single-family homes, townhouses, estates, sunrooms).
- Primary flow: Lead Contact & Appointment Request form capturing service selection (exterior, interior, screens, tracks), home type, estimated window count, address, preferred timing, and contact info.
- Click-to-call direct contact option.
- Interactive squeegee before/after glass demonstration showcasing streak-free results.
- Guaranteed 48-hour rain touch-up policy and $2M insurance protection badge.
