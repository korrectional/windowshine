## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: Vite + React with Tailwind CSS, Lucide icons, and static build for zero-config Vercel deployment.

## Users

Homeowners and residential property managers in Charlotte, NC and surrounding areas (Myers Park, Ballantyne, Dilworth, SouthPark, Plaza Midwood, Lake Norman, Matthews, Huntersville, Waxhaw) who want clean, bright exterior windows without having to take time off work or accommodate contractors inside their home.

## Product Purpose

WindowShine delivers exterior-only residential window cleaning, exterior screen washing, and sill wipe-downs in Charlotte, NC. The website exists to generate appointments and inquiries through clear communication, an interactive before-and-after demonstration, and a straightforward, zero-friction estimate request form.

## Positioning

100% exterior residential window cleaning. No interior intrusion, no need to be home during service. We wash exterior glass, clean exterior screens, and wipe down sills on single-family homes, townhouses, and multi-story estates across Charlotte.

## Operating Context

Charlotte homeowners browsing on phones or laptops, seeking clear windows after seasonal pollen or rain film; scheduling service without hassle.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Scope: Exterior residential only (no interior cleaning, no commercial).
- Water: Normal or purified water used depending on the job (no explicit deionized water claims).
- Guarantees & Insurance: No guarantees or insurance claims featured.
- Primary flow: Simple appointment / free estimate request form capturing home type, approximate window count, Charlotte neighborhood, street address, preferred timing, and contact info.
- Click-to-call direct contact option.
- Interactive squeegee before/after demonstration.

## Brand Commitments

- Brand Name: WindowShine
- Service Region: Charlotte, NC and Greater Mecklenburg County
- Tone: Dependable, crisp, direct, neighborly, professional
