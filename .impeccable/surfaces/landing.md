# Surface Brief: WindowShine Landing Page

Scope: Primary web landing page (`/`)
Visitor Mode: Persuade
Audience: Charlotte, NC homeowners seeking residential window cleaning
Job: Request a free estimate or schedule a window cleaning appointment
Proof & Content: Spotless residential photos, pure water technology explanation, 48-hour rain warranty, $2M insurance, transparent pricing guidelines, Charlotte neighborhood badges.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site rejects generic plumber/handyman clutter and blue-gradient templates. Instead, it embodies luminous architectural glass: deep navy dusk and brilliant sunlit cerulean accents, crystal-clear white space, squeegee-sharp precision, and immediate tactile proof of streak-free clarity.

OWN-WORLD: Color palette derived from optical glass and sky: Deep Architectural Slate (`#0B132B`, `#1C2541`), Daylight Cerulean (`#0077B6`, `#0096C7`), Foam White (`#FFFFFF`, `#F8FAFC`), and warm Sunburst highlight (`#F59E0B`). Crisp typography with balanced weights, real SVG icon line-work, and tactile squeegee interactive mechanics.

STORY: A Charlotte homeowner arrives, instantly sees real local residential work and our 100% Streak-Free & 48-Hour Rain Guarantees, interacts with a squeegee before/after reveal to experience the difference, selects their home size and service needs in an interactive estimate planner, and submits a fast appointment request.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-width hero anchored by the homeowner's actual work photo, a bold headline "Spotless Windows. Pure Carolina Light.", trust badges ($2M Insured, 48-Hr Rain Guarantee, Charlotte Local), and a direct dual CTA: "Request Your Free Estimate" and "Call (704) 555-SHINE".

FORM: Persuade Landing Page with interactive glass reveal, service matrix, local neighborhood trust map, and structured booking request form.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
